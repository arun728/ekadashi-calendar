# Custom product pages: marketing plan

A custom product page is an alternate App Store page with its own screenshots,
preview and promotional text, reached through its own link. Use the links in ads,
WhatsApp, YouTube and social posts; everyone else still sees the default page.
Apple allows up to 35, and each one has its own conversion numbers in App Analytics.

## Page 1 — हिंदी (North India) · ready

Most v1 downloads came from North India. Hindi speakers convert better when the
first screenshots are in Hindi, and Apple only shows a Hindi localization to people
whose App Store is set to Hindi.

- **Reference name:** Hindi – North India
- **Screenshots:** `hindi/` (10 slides, app in हिंदी, Hindi headlines), 6.9" set
- **Preview:** reuse the main preview for now; a Hindi cut is a good next step
- **Promotional text (170):** 2027 की हर एकादशी, आपके शहर के व्रत और पारण समय के साथ। पूरा पंचांग, सभी त्योहार, रिमाइंडर और आपकी व्रत यात्रा, अब हिंदी में।
- **Where to use the link:**
  - Apple Search Ads ad variations for Hindi keywords (एकादशी, व्रत, पंचांग, ekadashi vrat)
  - WhatsApp: temple, satsang and family groups; a forwardable message with the Hindi banner (`../../brand/banner-1920x1080-hi.png`)
  - YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels on Ekadashi eves, linking the page
  - Facebook groups for Hindu festivals and devotional communities
- **When to push:** the week before each big Ekadashi, matched to the In-App Events
  (Nirjala Jun 14, Devshayani Jul 14, Prabodhini Nov 10), plus the January
  new-year and October–November Diwali season peaks.
- **What to measure:** page impressions → downloads (conversion rate) against the
  default page; then trial → paid in App Store Connect.
