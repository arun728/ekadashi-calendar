Privacy Policy

Updated November 24, 2026 · Android, iPhone and iPad

Apptinel Software Private Limited publishes Ekadashi Calendar for Android, iPhone and iPad. Contact contact@apptinel.com for privacy questions. This policy covers the Android, iOS and iPadOS apps and these product pages. Availability of optional features depends on the installed version.

Calendar, Journey and settings

Your custom calendar entries, recent searches, vrat records and notes, progress, achievements, language, appearance, selected places and reminder preferences are stored on your device. The app shares the schedule and settings needed by its widgets with its widget extension through Apple’s App Group storage. On Android, widgets use locally stored schedule data. Android Auto Backup, Apple device backup or device transfer may retain or restore eligible app data depending on your device and account settings. The core features work without a separate Ekadashi Calendar account. The app has no ad network or in-app analytics service.

Location and device permissions

  • Location. If you allow location while using the app, it uses your platform’s location services to suggest the city and calculate fasting and Panchang times. It uses your selected or last available place, with Chennai as the default. A detected place can be saved on the device. The app may use Apple’s geocoder on iOS or the device’s geocoding provider on Android to look up a readable city name. That provider may process the location for the lookup. Android may offer approximate or precise location access. The app does not keep a movement history or send your location to a developer server. You can pick a place without granting permission.
  • Notifications. With your permission, the app schedules fasting, Parana and event reminders locally. You can change or turn off notification access in device Settings. Android may also require permission to schedule exact alarms for timely reminders; system battery restrictions can affect delivery.
  • Photos and sharing. If you save a card, the app may request permission to add the image to Photos on iOS. On Android it saves to your device’s image collection; older Android versions may request storage permission for saving images. When you share a card or recap, the system sends the content you select to the app or recipient you choose.

Optional Google Calendar import

If you choose Import from Google Calendar, the app uses Google Sign-In and the read-only Google Calendar permission (calendar.readonly). It can receive an account identifier and email to identify the selected account, list calendar names and identifiers, and read event information in the calendars and date range you select, such as titles, descriptions, locations and scheduled times. The app uses this information to display events, support search and reminders, and remember which calendars you selected. Imported events and selections are stored on your device. The app does not create, edit or delete events in Google Calendar or send imported event content to a developer server.

To verify eligibility for a free import across devices, the app sends your Google ID token over HTTPS to Firebase Authentication. Google authenticates the account and returns an account identifier and session credentials. The app uses the identifier to check or create a Firestore record with the imported month and a timestamp. The Firestore record itself contains no event content or email address. Firebase Authentication keeps the Google account's email address and account identifier with its sign-in record, so the free import is tied to that account. The registry exists to remember whether an account already used its free import.

Ekadashi Calendar’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google Calendar data to provide the import and related features you request. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Google Play and App Store purchases

Google Play processes Android purchases and Apple processes iOS purchases. The app receives purchase and entitlement information through Google Play Billing or StoreKit, such as product identifiers, purchase records and subscription status when available. It uses the store’s purchase information to grant or restore Premium, rather than a separate developer billing account. We do not receive your card or bank details through the app. Google describes its handling of account, payment and service data in the Google Privacy Policy. Apple describes its handling of account, payment and service data in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

  • Local app data. You can delete your own entries in the app. Deleting the app removes its local copy; Android also offers Clear storage in app settings. Eligible device backups or saved images may remain outside the app and can be managed in your backup settings, Photos or your Android image collection.
  • Google Calendar. In Calendar, Disconnect Google signs out and removes the imported copy and saved calendar selections for the connected account. You can also remove the app’s permission in your Google Account connections. Revoking access at Google blocks future access. To remove a copy already stored on the device, use Disconnect Google or delete the app.
  • Free-import record. The Firestore record is retained to enforce the one-time import across devices. Signing out or deleting the app does not remove it. To request deletion of the free-import record and the associated Firebase authentication record, email contact@apptinel.com. We may ask for information to verify the request. Never send a password or Google security code.
  • Support correspondence. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We keep the correspondence as needed to respond, follow up, or comply with legal obligations. You can contact us about access, correction or deletion requests.

This website

Our hosting provider processes standard request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect the site. The appearance setting is stored in your browser and the city lookup runs there against public catalog data. The city lookup does not request your current location or send your search terms. These pages have no advertising trackers or analytics. External services apply their own privacy policies.
