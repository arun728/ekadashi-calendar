Free features

Ekadashi Home

See dates for 2026 and 2027 in your city. Follow Smarta or Vaishnava calculations, see the fasting countdown and progress, and check the start and end of Parana. Open the festival detail for traditional stories, significance, fasting rules and food guidance. Save or share an Ekadashi card.

Calendar

Browse months, jump to a date, and add, edit or remove your own entries. See Ekadashis and imported events alongside your entries. Try one successful read-only Google Calendar import of the month you’re viewing. The free import is limited by your Google account and the app’s saved eligibility.

Journey

Record your first three new vrat entries and unlock your first three achievements. View and edit your recorded history, review statistics and streaks, and keep earned badges. The Journey overview shows progress by year, quarter and across years, with points and an optional year recap share card. Points track personal progress and have no cash value.

Panchang preview

Check the day’s tithi, lunar month and weekday, major observance names when available, sunrise and sunset, and moonrise and moonset. Browse Ekadashi dates in Key Days and the separate Panchang Ekadashi view.

Search

Find Ekadashis, personal calendar entries, and app screens from the toolbar. Use year and category filters and search with alternate spellings. Premium unlocks the full Panchang observance results.

Reminders and widgets

Set reminders for two days before, one day before, fasting start, and Parana. Add reminders for your own events. Choose Next Ekadashi or Upcoming Ekadashis for the Home Screen, or add Next Ekadashi to the Lock Screen. Widget previews are in Settings.

Appearance and language

Use light or dark appearance and the optional Glass Look. The app’s language setting also applies to dates, place names, Panchang terms, reminders and widgets.

Ekadashi Premium

Monthly and Yearly renew through the App Store. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Prices and applicable terms appear in your local checkout. Google sign-in is used for Calendar import. Premium purchase and restoration use your Apple Account.

Full Panchang
All five limbs with timings: tithi, vara, nakshatra, yoga and karana. See additional daily details, auspicious and inauspicious periods such as Abhijit Muhurta and Rahu Kalam, Muhurta and Rashi views, and the full festival and observance listings, search and related reminders.
Journey
Record more than three new vrats and unlock further streak, milestone and annual achievements as you meet their requirements.
Google Calendar import
Monthly and Yearly subscriptions can import 12 months of events starting from the subscription’s purchase month. Lifetime covers every supported Ekadashi calendar year. Import reads your selected Google calendars into the app.

Cities and time zones

Search 34,152 bundled cities worldwide. Each city has its own coordinates and IANA time zone, including local daylight-saving rules where applicable. The offline catalog offers 554 IANA time-zone IDs, including aliases, from release 2026b. Choose a city manually, use optional location detection, or enter custom coordinates and an available time zone in the app. Chennai is the starting fallback if no place is available. At locations or seasons where a sunrise or other event is unavailable, some timings may be unavailable.
