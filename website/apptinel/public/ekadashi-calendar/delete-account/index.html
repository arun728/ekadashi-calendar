Delete your account and data

Ekadashi Calendar by Apptinel Software Private Limited · Android, iPhone and iPad

Ekadashi Calendar works without an account. The only account record is created when you sign in with Google to import Google Calendar events: Firebase Authentication keeps your Google account identifier and email address, and a Firestore record notes the month of your one free import. You can ask us to delete both at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email contact@apptinel.com from the Google account you used in the app, with the subject “Delete my Ekadashi Calendar account”.
  2. If you write from another address, include the Google account email you signed in with. We may ask you to confirm that you own it. Never send a password or Google security code.
  3. We delete your Firebase Authentication record and your Firestore free-import record within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication sign-in record: the Google account identifier and email address.
  • Your Firestore free-import record: the imported month and its timestamp.

What is kept, and how to remove it yourself

  • Email correspondence. We keep your deletion request email as needed to show that we completed it and to meet legal obligations.
  • Data on your phone. Your calendar entries, vrat records, settings and imported Google events are stored only on your device. We never receive them. In the app, Disconnect Google in Calendar signs out and removes the imported events. Deleting the app, or Clear storage on Android, removes everything else.
  • Google access. You can remove the app’s Google Calendar permission in your Google Account connections.
  • Purchases. Premium subscriptions and purchases belong to your Google Play or App Store account, not to Ekadashi Calendar. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or in the App Store under your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Delete some data without deleting the account

You can delete any calendar entry or vrat record in the app, and Disconnect Google removes imported events from your device while keeping your sign-in record. To delete only the free-import record, email us as above and say so.
