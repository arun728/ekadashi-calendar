# Year rollover and store updates

Research snapshot of `dev` at `2d8f01b`. This draft is for review before implementation.

## What dev does today

- **Home (iOS and Android).** Both load all enabled years and use the Home city and tradition to select the first unfinished occurrence at launch, on a Home re-tap, and when the displayed current occurrence reaches Parana end. The Home list shows only one year and the automatic selection changes that year when necessary. The active Home re-tap keeps a fast visible through Parana. Returning from a different tab preserves its current card. Home's year menu lists 2026 then 2027.
- **Calendar.** Both support December and January within the same calendar, including the linked Parana entry on its actual local date. Calendar and Home share a concrete year in current code. Android observes changes to that year, while iOS uses the shared year when focusing Calendar and can set it when browsing months. A month/year wheel selects a concrete month and currently lists years oldest first.
- **Journey.** Overview, History, Statistics and Achievements use the shared Journey year menu. It already shows All years, then years descending, and supports history-only years. The underlying streak can span both years. The default year uses the device date, so test a device in a different year from the selected Home city around midnight.
- **Panchang.** Key days and Ekadashi browse a month. Daily, Muhurta and Rashi browse a day. All five share a date picker with a 1900–2100 range and use the Panchang location. The standalone Ekadashi calculation can already cross December into January. Key days also uses the Home occurrences for its Ekadashi entries.
- **Search and Settings.** Search already has All years followed by the enabled years in ascending order. Settings has no year scope menu. App reminders and native widgets receive the multi-year occurrence list and select from it. Test delivery around the boundary, including links to the right occurrence.
- **Update availability.** The Android production source is `1.1.1+4` and the audited `dev` snapshot is `2.0.0+5`. Neither has a Play in-app update client. The old Android `app_version` preference is local migration bookkeeping. Settings reads the installed version on both platforms. Native iOS has no store update check. The repository records v2 as the first public iOS release, so confirm the numeric App Store ID and storefronts before enabling future version checks.

## Proposed year workflow

1. Add a persisted All Years scope to Home and make it the default for automatic browsing. Keep the visible occurrence and its year separate from the selected filter. When opened during 2026, Home anchors on the current or next 2026 card while one exists. At the last local Parana end, advance to the first 2027 card and keep All Years selected. Keep the pager chronological across years and render it efficiently.
2. Give year-scope menus a shared ordering rule. Show localized All Years first, then 2027, then 2026. Choosing 2026 or 2027 browses that year. Preserve deliberate archive browsing and occurrence links. Retain the active Home re-tap's existing current/next rule, including Parana and its escape from a past year. Confirm that returning from another tab continues to restore its current card. Treat the Calendar month/year wheel and Panchang graphical date picker as date navigation. Show newest year first in the Calendar wheel and retain the full date range in the Panchang picker.
3. Preserve explicit Calendar dates through automatic Home rollover. Keep numeric year navigation coherent with Calendar and let Calendar Today focus the Home city's current date. Do not let the Calendar's viewed year replace All Years. Keep Search and Journey filters independent of Home. Reuse existing translated All years copy and check the longer labels in the Home grid.
4. Use the enabled-year manifest for subsequent releases. Add validated 2028, 2029 and 2030 as they ship on both platforms. Derive UI order without changing chronological calculation or history logic. Exercise the end of the last shipped year explicitly so an old card can be recognized as archived and later years appear after an app update. Preserve occurrence IDs, widget links, reminder deduplication, history and rewards already earned.

## Proposed app update workflow

- **Android v1 to v2 at launch.** Check Google Play Console's Recovery tools in App bundle explorer for the v1 production bundle. Google can show a full-screen, dismissible update when targeted users launch an already-published v1. Acceptance lets Play update to the latest compatible version and dismissal allows use until the next cold launch. Confirm Play App Signing, Android App Bundle, recovery eligibility, the higher compatible v2 version code, and a newer version in all relevant tracks. During staged rollout, availability follows Play eligibility. Test the recovery preview before any production action. If ineligible, publish an updater-enabled v1 bridge before v2 if release timing allows. Users still on older binaries have ordinary Play updates, and bridge adopters get the in-app prompt. [Play Console recovery documentation](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13812041)
- **Android future releases.** Ship the official Play In-App Updates API in v2 via the Kotlin/Flutter bridge. On launch and foreground, query Play asynchronously with a cooldown. Show a translated, dismissible Update available prompt when eligible. Update now opens Play's confirmation over the app. In the flexible flow, continue using the app during download, then offer Restart to update and recover a completed download after relaunch. Let Play install and restart. Reserve immediate mode for a separately approved critical update. [Play implementation guide](https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-updates/kotlin-java)
- **iOS future releases.** Put a dismissible update prompt in the first App Store build. Use a storefront-aware availability check with the real Apple app ID and numeric version comparison. Validate the installed OS is supported and treat missing or unavailable data as no prompt. Evaluate Apple's public lookup against the intended storefronts before choosing it as the signal. Open the public App Store product page when the user chooses to update. Apple handles installation and auto-updates. The app has no public Play-style in-app binary install API. [Apple product page guidance](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/qa/qa1629/_index.html) · [Apple lookup examples](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/AudioVideo/Conceptual/iTuneSearchAPI/LookupExamples.html) · [Apple phased release guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/update-your-app/release-a-version-update-in-phases)
- Localize all app-owned strings in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati and Bengali. Play and Apple own their dialog copy and locale. Check app and store surfaces separately.
