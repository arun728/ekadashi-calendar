## What Free and Premium include

Free use includes the core Ekadashi schedule, Calendar and custom entries, search, reminders, widgets, the Panchang preview, and up to three new Journey entries and three achievement unlocks. Viewing or editing your existing Journey records remains available. Premium adds the full Panchang, expanded observance search and reminders, further Journey recording and achievement unlocks, and wider Google Calendar import.

The free Google Calendar import covers one successful import of the viewed month, subject to the one-time eligibility check. An active subscription allows a 12-month import window based on its purchase month. Lifetime covers all years supported by the app’s Ekadashi calendar. Imports require a Google account and approved read access. The app shows a copy of the selected events. It has no two-way sync and changes nothing in your source calendars. The app can remove Premium-imported copies when Play reports that the subscription is no longer owned. Free imports and your personal vrat history are separate.

Journey points and badges represent personal progress. They carry no cash value or purchase credit.
